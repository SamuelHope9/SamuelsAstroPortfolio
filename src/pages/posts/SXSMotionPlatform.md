#SXS Motion platform

This project features a side-by-side (SxS) vehicle integrated with a motion platform, allowing users to drive while immersed in a VR headset.  
The SXS simulator was designed for safety training in hazardous work environments.  
Since this took place shortly after the lockdowns, and with some team members stuck in China, I was the sole person working on the project developed for WorkSafe.  
 
##Motion control software
One of the components I developed for this project was the motion control software, which was well beyond my skill set at the time. 
I transitioned from Unreal Engine development to writing C code to control industrial servo motor controllers The communication was done using binary over a network cable.   
Even getting the servos to turn on required a quadruple handshake.  
Information on the internet was barely existant, I had to rely on the documentation for the servors which were well over 1000 pages.  
  
  
##Overcoming Obstacles Under Tight Deadlines.
This was a highly ambitious project with tight deadlines. On top of that, we outsourced some work that was never delivered, that was needed to complete a large portion of the project.  
Just over two weeks before the deadline, we found out the outsourced wasnt being done even with repitave assurances.  
To make sure we met the deadline, Through share persevarance and will i manage to complete the project albiet some significant overtime.  
  
Finishing the project on time felt like a miracle, but it was ultimately a success.  
It was voted one of the most fun attractions at Field Days, second only to the tractor pull.   
This was also the first time a government department had ever made the list of top five things to do at field days.
